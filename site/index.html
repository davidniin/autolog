Private by design

AutoLog isn't a service you sign up for: everyone runs their own copy. There is no central server, no account and no analytics.

  • Your data lives in your PostgreSQL and on your disk.
  • Invoice photos are read on the phone; they're never sent anywhere.
  • Password-protected, with lock-out of repeated attempts and strict security headers.
  • Open source (MIT): you can read every line.

Install yours in minutes

One Docker container plus PostgreSQL. At home, on a NAS, a Raspberry Pi, a VPS or any cloud.

  1. Download and configuregit clone https://github.com/<owner>/autolog.git && cd autolog
    cp .env.example .env
  2. Start itdocker compose up -d --build
  3. Add HTTPS and open it on your phoneWith Tailscale, Caddy or Cloudflare Tunnel. Then "Add to Home Screen".
