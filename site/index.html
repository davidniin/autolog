Private by design

AutoLog isn't a service you sign up for. There is no account, no central server and no analytics: your data is only yours.

  • Your data is saved on your phone (or on your own server if you prefer). We never see it.
  • Invoice photos are read on the phone; they're never sent anywhere.
  • If you set it up on a server, it is protected with a password and blocks repeated attempts.
  • Open source (MIT): you can read every line.

Three ways to start: pick yours

You don't need to be technical. Start on your phone in seconds and, if you ever want to, move to your own space later.
